# TuniDrive asset provenance

All runtime imagery is local in `public/assets/`. SVG artwork is original programmatic artwork. WebP images were created using the built-in Imagegen tool, then encoded as WebP with Pillow. No external image URLs or image-generation API keys are needed to run the project.

Generated images are illustrative, not actual fleet/location photography. Model renders can contain generated inaccuracies. Non-model SVG fleet artwork is labelled as category illustration.

Generated asset prompts:

- `hero.webp`: Premium wide automotive photograph: silver unbranded SUV on a coastal road near Sidi Bou Said, turquoise Mediterranean, whitewashed buildings, golden afternoon light, vehicle on right and open space on left, no text or watermark.
- `airport.webp`: Premium automotive travel photograph: silver compact sedan at a modern Tunisian airport arrivals curb, terminal and palms, car on right, negative space on left, golden afternoon sunlight, no readable signage or watermark.
- `luxury.webp`: Elegant black luxury sedan outside a white Mediterranean coastal boutique resort in Tunisia, palms and sea at dusk, car on right, cinematic light, no text or watermark.
- `clio.webp`: Product photograph of a 2025 Renault Clio, deep Mediterranean blue, front three-quarter view facing left, whole car on a pale grey seamless studio background, premium automotive lighting, no text or watermark.
- `duster.webp`: Product photograph of a 2025 Dacia Duster, pearl white, front three-quarter view facing left, whole car on a pale grey studio background, soft automotive lighting, no text or watermark.
- `mercedes.webp`: Product photograph of a 2025 Mercedes-Benz C-Class, silver, front three-quarter view facing left, whole car on a pale grey studio background, soft automotive lighting, no text or watermark.
- `hammamet.webp`: Editorial travel photograph of Hammamet, white medina walls and coastal kasbah, turquoise sea, bougainvillea, warm afternoon light and azure sky, no text or watermark.
- `djerba.webp`: Editorial travel photograph of Djerba, white domed traditional mosque architecture, palms, sandy path and turquoise water, warm morning light, no text or watermark.

Original vector assets:

- `logo.svg`, `logo-white.svg`, `logo-mobile.svg`, `icon.svg`, and `../favicon.svg`: TuniDrive TD/road-inspired mark with navy and coral colour system.
- `vehicle-1.svg` through `vehicle-20.svg`: category illustrations, not exact model representations.
- `location-1.svg` through `location-9.svg`: stylized Tunisian coastal destination illustrations.

Lucide supplies interface icons under its package license. The site uses system typography so that it works without third-party font requests. Customer avatars are fictional initials rendered by the UI.
